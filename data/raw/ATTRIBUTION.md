# Data Sources & Attribution

Luminalix's `regional_survey_data` combines real, licensed public health data
with fully synthetic data. This file documents exactly which is which, per
DataHub Agent Hackathon rules requiring accurate licensing disclosure for
any bring-your-own data in an Apache 2.0 submission.

## Real data (licensed, redistributed with attribution)

### COVID-19
Edouard Mathieu, Hannah Ritchie, Lucas Rodés-Guirao, Cameron Appel, Daniel Gavrilov,
Charlie Giattino, Joe Hasell, Bobbie Macdonald, Saloni Dattani, Diana Beltekian,
Esteban Ortiz-Ospina, and Max Roser (2020) – "COVID-19 Pandemic". Data adapted from
World Health Organization. Retrieved from Our World in Data
(https://ourworldindata.org/coronavirus). **Licensed under CC BY 4.0.**

### Tuberculosis
Global Tuberculosis Report 2025. Geneva: World Health Organization; 2025.
Data adapted via Our World in Data
(https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/number-of-tuberculosis-cases).
**Licensed under CC BY 4.0.**

### Malaria (case-count indicator only)
World Health Organization (Global Health Observatory), via World Bank (2026) –
processed by Our World in Data. "New cases of malaria per 1,000 people at risk"
[dataset]. Retrieved from
https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/incidence-of-malaria. **Licensed under CC BY 4.0.**

Note: only this specific case-count indicator was used. OWID's malaria
*deaths* and *prevalence* indicators are sourced from IHME's Global Burden
of Disease study, which is explicitly marked non-redistributable — those
were deliberately excluded.

### Influenza
World Health Organization. FluNet (Global Influenza Virological Surveillance).
Data provided by National Influenza Centres (NICs) of the Global Influenza
Surveillance and Response System (GISRS). https://www.who.int/tools/flunet
Used under the WHO Policy on the Use and Sharing of Data (non-commercial,
not-for-profit use).

## Synthetic data (not derived from any redistributed real dataset)

### HIV
Fully synthetic. No real dataset was used or redistributed — OWID's HIV
indicators are sourced from IHME's Global Burden of Disease study, which
does not permit redistribution, so this disease was excluded from the real
data and modeled synthetically instead. Values are loosely modeled on
publicly known regional HIV prevalence patterns for narrative plausibility
only, not derived from or copied from any specific dataset.

### Ebola
Fully synthetic. Regions and relative case-rate patterns are loosely modeled
on the real, ongoing 2026 Bundibugyo virus disease outbreak in the
Democratic Republic of Congo and Uganda (declared a Public Health Emergency
of International Concern by WHO on 16 May 2026), for narrative grounding
only. No structured dataset was scraped or redistributed.

### Lassa fever
Fully synthetic. Regions are loosely modeled on real 2026 Nigeria Centre
for Disease Control (NCDC) situation reports, which identified Ondo,
Bauchi, Edo, Taraba, and Benue states as accounting for the large majority
of confirmed 2026 cases, for narrative grounding only. No structured
dataset was scraped or redistributed.

## hospital_admissions and resource_allocation datasets

Both datasets are entirely synthetic, generated by scripts in
`data/ingestion/`, with no third-party data or licensing considerations.
